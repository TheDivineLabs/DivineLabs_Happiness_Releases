Installation, updates, repair and removal now share a wizard with MusicWars artwork, light and dark themes, and Russian or English. Open it from the Maintenance button in the application.

You can verify files and restore damaged components. Normal removal keeps settings; full removal is a separate choice.

The build is available locally for testing. Public downloads have not been replaced yet.
