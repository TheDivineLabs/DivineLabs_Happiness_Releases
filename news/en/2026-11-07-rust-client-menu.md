# New menu and metrics in Rust Client 0.1.1

2026-11-07 · 0.1.1-rust

Click the DivineLabs mark to open the home panel with the version status and shortcuts to logs, release notes and support. In Client settings, you can add window and game screenshot buttons beside the mark, assign keyboard shortcuts and enable Imgur uploads. Graphics settings have their own section.

Move the panel separately by dragging its header. Ctrl+Shift+R lets you select a screenshot region. Logs are grouped by date and run, with Russian messages and timestamps like `[15:34:26]`. Files older than seven days are compressed automatically.

The home card shows “Client ∞” and takes up less space when the client is up to date.

Client settings now include a draggable panel for memory, CPU usage and TCP latency to the MusicWars website. Each metric can be enabled separately. Total system usage appears as `12GB/16GB` and `99%/100%`.

Reload the game from Home, the metrics settings, or a configurable keyboard shortcut.
