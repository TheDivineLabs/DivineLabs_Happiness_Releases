# ClientPlus r4: combined optimization test

2026-11-01 · 0.2.11

The private Preview now contains the combined ClientPlus r4 test build: animation/status label caching, shared label tables and overlapping mass-effect reduction. The three modes can be toggled independently.

The isolated fixture verified completion of 300 mass effects, 60 proton explosions and 10 shield animations. No live heavy-battle FPS comparison is available yet. Overlapping brightness may differ in the experimental mode.

This is a diagnostic development build for local testing, with automated gameplay blocked. Public Client remains at version 0.2.11; public Plus binaries and subscriptions have not been released.
